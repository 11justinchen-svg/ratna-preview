Lab-grown

Same diamond.
Kinder price.

  1. Grown, not mined.

    Pure carbon, grown in weeks under conditions that recreate how diamonds form in the earth.

  2. Graded the same way.

    Chemically and optically identical to mined diamonds, and graded on the same scale by labs such as IGI and GIA.

  3. More stone for your budget.

    A lab-grown diamond usually costs far less than a mined one of the same size and grade.
